Documentation

System context and operational architecture for the Parking Management System.

Context & Problem Statement

Urban areas face a critical shortage of parking efficiency due to increasing vehicle density. Traditional manual systems lead to unorganized slot allocation, congestion, and a complete lack of data-driven insights.

Primary GoalAutomation
TargetUrban Traffic
Tech StackSQL / Python

Core Operations

Lifecycle Management

Automated control of entry/exit with precise timestamps for active parking sessions.

Dynamic Allocation

Real-time assignment of available slots to minimize driver search time and congestion.

Automated Billing

Charge calculation based on duration, reducing human error and increasing transparency.

Relational Database Logic

All operations are anchored in a structured relational database. Every entry or exit triggers an immediate atomic update to ensure consistency.

  • Real-time slot availability tracking
  • Structured record-keeping for audit trails
  • Reduced human dependency in data entry
